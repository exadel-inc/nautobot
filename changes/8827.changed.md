Changed `IPAddress.dns_name` validation to enforce DNS label structure: labels must be 1-63 alphanumeric or hyphen characters, must not begin or end with a hyphen, and must not be empty, and the total length must not exceed 253 characters (a single trailing dot is allowed). Underscores are no longer allowed. Existing records with invalid values are not modified, but must be corrected the next time they are edited.
